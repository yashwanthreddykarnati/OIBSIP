01

About His Life

A scientist who inspired a nation and a teacher who encouraged millions of young minds to dream bigger.

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and statesman who served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007.

Born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, Kalam came from a modest background. His determination and passion for learning helped him build an extraordinary career in India's space and defence programmes.

He played an important role in India's civilian space programme and military missile development efforts. His contribution to India's missile and launch vehicle programmes earned him the popular title "Missile Man of India."

Even after serving as President, Kalam continued to connect with students and young people. He believed education, creativity and perseverance could transform the future of India.
